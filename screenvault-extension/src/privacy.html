ScreenVault

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Summary

ScreenVault records your screen and uploads videos to your own Google Drive. We never store your video content. We only store the metadata needed to share and manage your recordings.

What we collect

  • Google account info — your email address and display name, used to identify you and link your recordings to your workspace.
  • Video metadata — title, duration, and workspace association. The video file itself lives in your Google Drive, not on our servers.
  • Usage stats — recording counts and timestamps, used to enforce fair-use limits (e.g. 100 recordings/day).
  • Playback events — anonymous events (play started, play failed) for product reliability. No viewer identity is recorded.
  • Debug logs — technical error logs to help us fix bugs.

What we do NOT collect

  • Your video content — it goes directly to your Google Drive.
  • Your full Google Drive contents — we only access files that ScreenVault creates (drive.file scope).
  • Browsing history or tab content.
  • Payment information.

Google Drive access

We request the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • drive.file — create and read files that ScreenVault creates. We cannot access any other files in your Drive.
  • drive.metadata.readonly — read your Drive storage quota to show how much space is available.
  • userinfo.email and userinfo.profile — identify you by email and display name.

Data retention

  • Video metadata — retained until you delete the recording.
  • Debug logs — automatically deleted after 14 days.
  • Playback metrics — automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Auth token cache — expires after 10 minutes.

Data storage & infrastructure

Video files are stored in your Google Drive account. Metadata is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database (USA). Auth tokens are temporarily cached in Cloudflare KV with a 10-minute TTL.

Sharing

When you share a recording link, it becomes viewable by anyone with that link. The link contains an encrypted file identifier — it cannot be guessed. You can revoke access at any time by deleting the file from your Google Drive or deleting the recording from your ScreenVault library.

Third-party services

  • Google Drive API — for video storage and sharing.
  • Cloudflare Workers & D1 — for our backend API and metadata storage.

Your rights

  • Delete any recording from your library at any time — this removes it from our database and from Google Drive.
  • Disconnect your Google account from the extension at any time.
  • Request deletion of all your data by emailing us.
