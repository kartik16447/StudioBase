Foyr Screen Recorder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Summary

Foyr Screen Recorder records your screen and uploads videos to our own Cloudflare storage, scoped to your workspace. We use Google Sign-In only to verify who you are — not to access your Google Drive or any other Google data.

What we collect

  • Google account info — your email address and display name, used to identify you and link your recordings to your workspace.
  • Video metadata — title, duration, and workspace association.
  • Usage stats — recording counts and timestamps, used to enforce fair-use limits (e.g. 100 recordings/day).
  • Playback events — anonymous events (play started, play failed) for product reliability. No viewer identity is recorded.
  • Debug logs — technical error logs to help us fix bugs.

What we do NOT collect

  • Your Google Drive contents, or any other Google data beyond your email and display name.
  • Browsing history or tab content.
  • Payment information.

Google account access

We request the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • userinfo.email and userinfo.profile — identify you by email and display name.

Data retention

  • Video metadata — retained until you delete the recording.
  • Debug logs — automatically deleted after 14 days.
  • Playback metrics — automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Auth token cache — expires after 10 minutes.

Data storage & infrastructure

Video files and metadata are stored in Cloudflare R2 and D1. Auth tokens are temporarily cached in Cloudflare KV with a 10-minute TTL.

Sharing

When you share a recording link, it becomes viewable by anyone with that link. The link contains an encrypted file identifier — it cannot be guessed. You can revoke access at any time by deleting the recording from your Foyr Screen Recorder library.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare Workers, R2 & D1 — for our backend API, video storage, and metadata storage.

Your rights

  • Delete any recording from your library at any time — this removes it from our database and storage.
  • Disconnect your Google account from the extension at any time.
  • Request deletion of all your data by emailing us.
