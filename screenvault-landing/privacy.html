Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

RecVault collects your Google account email and name for authentication. We store a reference to your Google Drive file IDs so we can generate shareable playback links. We do not store the video content itself — all recordings live exclusively in your own Google Drive.

How we use your data

  • Authentication via Google OAuth
  • Generating and serving shareable recording links
  • Workspace membership and collaboration features
  • Product analytics (anonymous usage metrics)

Your Google Drive data

RecVault uses Google Drive only to upload recordings on your behalf, into a folder you choose. We request the drive.file scope — meaning we can only access files that RecVault itself creates, not your broader Drive.

Data retention

You can delete recordings from your dashboard at any time. Deleting a recording removes our database reference; the file in Google Drive is also permanently deleted. Waitlist emails are retained until the waitlist is closed or you request removal.

Third parties

We use Cloudflare for infrastructure (Workers, D1 database, KV). No video data is shared with any third party. We do not sell your data.
