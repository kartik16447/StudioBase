Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

Foyr Screen Recorder collects your Google account email and name for authentication. Recordings are stored in our own Cloudflare R2 storage, associated with your workspace.

How we use your data

  • Authentication via Google OAuth
  • Generating and serving shareable recording links
  • Workspace membership and collaboration features
  • Product analytics (anonymous usage metrics)

Your Google account data

Foyr Screen Recorder uses Google Sign-In only to verify your identity — we request the email and profile scopes only, and do not access your Google Drive or any other Google data.

Data retention

You can delete recordings from your dashboard at any time. Deleting a recording removes our database reference and permanently deletes the underlying file from our storage.

Third parties

We use Cloudflare for infrastructure (Workers, D1 database, KV, R2). No video data is shared with any third party. We do not sell your data.
